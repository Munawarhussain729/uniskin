Add `ui-table` and `ui-table-wrap` to the vocabulary (v1.4): native `<table>` styling via nested `th`/`td`/`tr`/`caption` selectors, `ui-soft` zebra, `ui-outline` cell grid, `ui-ghost` line-less, intents tinting header underline + selected-row fill, `ui-sm`/`ui-lg` density, `[aria-sort]` arrows, sticky `thead th`, tr-level selected/disabled and table-level `ui-loading`/`aria-busy`. Component-scoped hooks `--ui-table-*` documented in `docs/writing-adapters.md`. shadcn maps to `Table` (`border-b`, `hover:bg-muted/50`, selected `--muted`); daisyUI maps to `table`/`table-zebra`.
