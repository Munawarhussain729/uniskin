Add a drawer as a placement of `ui-modal`, with no new class: `data-placement="start|end|top|bottom"` on the modal docks the box to that edge and slides it in. `start` and `end` follow the writing direction; side drawers are full height and `--ui-modal-w` wide, top and bottom drawers are full width up to `--ui-drawer-h`. The slide is off under reduced motion. The open mechanisms, backdrop and the `<dialog>` guidance are the modal's own.
