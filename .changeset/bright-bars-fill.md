Add `ui-progress` (vocabulary v1.6): a progress bar styled on the native `<progress>` element, with intents, `ui-outline` and `ui-ghost` styles and `ui-sm`/`ui-lg` sizes. An indeterminate `<progress>` shows a sliding bar, replaced by a dim full-width bar under `prefers-reduced-motion`. The bar keeps at least 3:1 against its track in all three skins in light and dark. Component-scoped hooks `--ui-progress-*` are documented in `docs/writing-adapters.md`; shadcn maps to `Progress` and daisyUI to `progress`.
