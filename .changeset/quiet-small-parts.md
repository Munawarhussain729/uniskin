Add `ui-avatar`, `ui-skeleton` and `ui-separator`. The avatar is a circle for initials, an icon or an image, with intents, `ui-soft`, `ui-outline` and three sizes. The skeleton is a pulsing placeholder block that stops under reduced motion. The separator is a 1px rule, horizontal by default and vertical on `aria-orientation="vertical"`. Hooks `--ui-avatar-*`, `--ui-skeleton-*` and `--ui-separator-*` are documented in `docs/writing-adapters.md`; shadcn and daisyUI map the avatar fallback and the skeleton fill.
