Add `ui-breadcrumb`, `ui-pagination` and `ui-pagination-item`. The breadcrumb goes on the `<ol>` of a labelled `<nav>`, with decorative separators (empty alt text) and a bold `aria-current="page"` item; `ui-sm` and `ui-lg` set the size. The pagination goes on a `<ul>`: intents on the list fill the current page (`aria-current="page"` or `ui-active`), sizes set the item size, and items take `ui-disabled`. Hooks `--ui-breadcrumb-*` and `--ui-pagination-*` are in `docs/writing-adapters.md`.
