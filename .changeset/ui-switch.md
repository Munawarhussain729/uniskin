Add `ui-switch` (vocabulary v1.2): an on/off toggle drawn on a native `<input type="checkbox" role="switch">` or a headless `<button role="switch" aria-checked>`, with the same intents, styles, sizes and states as `ui-checkbox`, in the default theme and both adapters. The off track and thumb keep 3:1 contrast against the page surface in light and dark, the thumb travels with logical properties so it flips in right-to-left layouts, and transitions respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `forced-colors`. Per-component hooks: `--ui-switch-size`, `--ui-switch-track`, `--ui-switch-thumb`, `--ui-switch-pad`.
