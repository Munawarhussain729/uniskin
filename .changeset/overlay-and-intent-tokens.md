New optional tokens, all defaulting to today's look: `--ui-overlay` and `--ui-overlay-soft` for the modal scrim and soft veil (light and dark sets), and per-intent pins `--ui-<intent>-hover`, `--ui-<intent>-border` and `--ui-<intent>-soft-hover` (exposed as `--ui-c-hover`, `--ui-c-border`, `--ui-c-soft-hover` on every intent). Scale-driven adapters can now remap tokens instead of rewriting button style slots.
