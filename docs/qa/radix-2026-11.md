# @uniskin/radix QA (November 2026)

Screenshots: `docs/qa/radix/`: an overview at 360, 768 and 1280 px in
light and dark, plus the menu, avatar and skeleton, breadcrumb and
pagination, slider and drawer sections. Checked by the shared specs, which
now run a fourth skin: unit tests, axe, contrast, keyboard, motion, and the
per-component specs (field, radio, switch, table, tooltip, toast, menu,
slider, drawer, nav, small), in light and dark.

## Coverage

`checkAdapter(adapter)` reports **297 of 297** combinations, no components
missing, no unknown slots; a unit test pins it. For comparison the shadcn and
daisyUI adapters define 108 and 138 of the 297 and fall back to the default
skin for the rest.

## Where Radix Themes' own values did not pass, and what the adapter does

Radix's scales are tuned for APCA contrast, which is more lenient than WCAG's
ratio in places.

| Radix | Problem | Adapter |
|-------|---------|---------|
| Solid green step 9, white text | 3.1:1 | Step 11 in light; in dark, dark text on step 9 |
| Solid red step 9, white text | 4.0:1 | Step 11 in light; in dark, dark text on step 10 |
| Soft success text (step 11 on step 3) | 4.2:1 | Step 12 |
| Soft and plain warning text (step 11) | 4.2 and 4.4:1 | Step 12 |
| Text-field border `gray-a7` | about 1.7:1 | 55% mix of the text colour |
| Focus outline step 8 | about 2.4:1 | Solid step 9 (11 in dark), after a gap |
| Secondary text with a fixed light-mode grey | 3.0:1 in dark | A 68% mix of the text colour, so it follows the mode |

The fallbacks are the Radix Themes 3 default `blue`, `violet`, `green`, `amber`,
`red` and `gray` values; the playground is checked with them.
