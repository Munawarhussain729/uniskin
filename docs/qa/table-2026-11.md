# ui-table — QA notes (2026-11)

Measured with `inspectElement`/canvas-resolved colours in the playground,
all three adapters × light/dark. Screenshots in `docs/qa/table/`
(1280/768/360 × light/dark × default/shadcn/daisyui, plus `-hover`).

## Passes

- `th`/`td`/`caption` text: ≥ 4.5:1 in all 6 skin/mode combos
  (`e2e/table.spec.ts`).
- Header underline (`thead th` 2px border): ≥ 3:1 in all 6. Drawn as
  `color-mix(--ui-c 55%, --ui-surface-fg)` — keeps the intent tint while
  staying legible; a bare bright intent colour (e.g. `ui-warning`)
  would not reach 3:1.
- Selected-row fill (`--ui-c-soft`) distinct from plain rows in all 6;
  selected text: default/shadcn = `--ui-c-soft-fg`/surface fg, daisyUI
  = surface fg (see below).
- `ui-soft` zebra: distinct fill, text ≥ 4.5:1 on the stripe.
- `ui-outline` cell grid borders render; `ui-ghost` removes all lines
  except the (intent-tinted) header underline.
- Wrap focus ring ≥ 3:1 in all 6 (`ui-table-wrap[tabindex="0"]`).
- `[aria-sort]` `::after` triangle renders (CSS shape, no icon font);
  `thead th` is `position: sticky` inside the wrap.
- `ui-loading`/`aria-busy` pulses `tbody`; stops under
  `prefers-reduced-motion` (asserted in `motion.spec.ts` + table spec).
- `prefix: "uk"` renames `uk-table`, `uk-table-wrap`, nested
  `tr.uk-active`/`tr.uk-disabled` selector references and `uk-pulse`
  (`tests/skin.test.ts`; required an `emit.ts` fix — nested selector
  keys were never re-prefixed before).

## Recorded non-conformances

- **`tbody tr` row dividers are subtle by design** — measured:
  default light 1.33:1, default dark ~1.3:1, shadcn ~1.3:1 (upstream
  `border-b` parity), daisyUI ~1.5:1 (bumped from ~1.0 after QA — the
  `base-300` colour was invisible in dark). These are decorative
  separators, not the component's structural cue; structure is carried
  by the ≥3:1 header underline and (under `ui-soft`) the zebra fill.
  Asserted only as "present" in `e2e/table.spec.ts`.
- **daisyUI selected-row text**: `--ui-c-soft-fg` on `--ui-c-soft`
  measured 4.47 (light danger) and 4.17 (dark primary) — adapter now
  keeps the tint but renders selected-row text in `--ui-surface-fg`
  (≥9:1), matching the shadcn mapping.
- **axe**: `color-contrast` nodes on `table.ui-loading td` /
  `table[aria-busy] td` are allow-listed — axe samples mid-animation
  and reports indeterminate contrast; static contrast is asserted in
  `table.spec.ts`.
- **Caption** sits below the table (`caption-side: bottom`, shadcn
  convention) at ~65% fg — ≥4.5:1 everywhere measured.
