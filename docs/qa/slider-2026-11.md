# ui-slider QA (November 2026)

Screenshots: `docs/qa/slider/`, three skins by light and dark by 360, 768
and 1280 px. Checked by `apps/playground/e2e/slider.spec.ts` in all six
skin and mode combinations, plus the shared axe and unit tests.

| Check | Result |
|-------|--------|
| Arrow keys and End change the value; the demo's `oninput` updates the fill | passes |
| A focused slider shows a >= 2px ring | passes |
| Sizes are ordered | passes |
| A disabled slider is dimmed and not focusable | passes |
| Thumb colour >= 3:1 against the page (WCAG 1.4.11) | passes in all six combinations |
| axe, six combinations | passes |

## Findings

- **The filled track.** Chromium and WebKit expose no pseudo-element for the
  progress side of a range track (Firefox has `::-moz-range-progress`), so a
  single, consistent look needs the fill painted as a gradient from
  `--ui-slider-fill`. That is one line of script, documented; without it the
  slider is unfilled and fully usable.
- **Selector lists.** The `-webkit-slider-*` and `-moz-range-*` selectors
  cannot share a rule (an unknown pseudo-element invalidates the whole
  list), so each is its own rule; `writing-adapters.md` says so.
- **Not tested here.** Firefox rendering is not covered by the Chromium
  e2e run; the Firefox selectors are asserted in the unit tests only.
